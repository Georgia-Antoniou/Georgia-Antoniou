Travel e-commerce site

Manual QA Tester

For the Agile Travel (Demonstration Project), I performed manual exploratory testing to evaluate the web application’s quality and functionality. I systematically explored the application to uncover bugs and design flaws, which I then formally documented into detailed test cases and bug reports. My work demonstrated my proficiency in both creative defect discovery and structured documentation, providing a clear record of my testing process and contributions to the project's quality in a professional setting.

My Responsibilities:

  • Conducted manual exploratory testing across the Agile Travel application to uncover bugs and potential user experience flaws.
  • Authored test cases based on exploratory findings, creating a structured and reusable test suite.
  • Documented and reported all discovered defects in a bug-tracking system, including steps to reproduce, expected vs. actual results, and supporting screenshots.
  • Assessed the application's overall functionality and usability to ensure a high-quality user experience.
