AI Platform Testing

Manual QA Tester

As a Beta Tester and UAT Specialist for the Socra AI platform, I executed exploratory testing to evaluate the product's quality and user experience. Without formal requirements, I acted as a real user, creatively navigating the platform to uncover bugs, identify usability issues, and assess its overall readiness for launch. This approach allowed me to discover critical defects and provide valuable user-centric feedback that directly influenced the final product.

My Responsibilities:

  • Identified and documented a wide range of defects, from critical crashes to minor usability issues, using screen captures and detailed steps to reproduce.
  • Assessed the user flow and intuitiveness of the AI platform from an end-user perspective, providing actionable feedback to the development and design teams.
  • Contributed to the overall quality and stability of the product by proactively testing new features and their interactions with existing functionalities.
  • Provided qualitative feedback on the platform's performance, stability, and reliability under various real-world usage scenarios.
